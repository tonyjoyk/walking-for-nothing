# Story 2.9: A quiet hint that he responds

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** a subtle sign that the man responds to me,
**so that** I discover the greeting without being told.

## Acceptance criteria

1. While the mouse pointer is on him, the cursor is a minimal hand. Off
   him, and over the speech bubble, it's normal. On touch screens there's
   no change.
2. While the visitor hovers him, or he's greeting them, and Juhu is with
   him, she stops with him a little further back. She looks up at his
   face, ears forward, with her tail raised and wagging. It eases in and
   out, and never snaps.
3. When the visitor leaves and he walks on, she relaxes and walks with him
   again.
4. Tests and the build pass. Juhu's existing tests still pass (she waits
   while he stands; her feet are planted).
