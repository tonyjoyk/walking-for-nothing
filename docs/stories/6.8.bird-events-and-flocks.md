# Story 6.8: Bird events and flocks

## Status

Draft

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** birds calling now and then, and a flock call when birds fly past,
**so that** the scene feels alive without becoming busy or repetitive.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each scene has event pools with a mean interval and minimum gap (Dawn 6 s, Day 14 s, Windy 30 s, Sunset 12 s, Night 25 s), with no birds in Rain.
2. Intervals follow a Poisson process from a seeded random generator.
3. The same file never plays twice in a row.
4. Each play varies: gain ±3 dB, rate 0.96–1.04, pan −0.6 to 0.6.
5. When the visual birds enter, a flock call plays, panned from that side, replacing the synthetic chirps.
