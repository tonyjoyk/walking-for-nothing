# Story 1.3: Picker: labelled options, a menu button, a looping pick

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** to see clearly what each scene option is, move between scenes
freely, and have my choice stay,
**so that** I can settle into the time of day I like.

## Owner feedback (15 Nov 2026)

- The icons aren't clear, so put the scene's name inside each option.
- Closed, the button should look like a menu (hamburger). Open, it becomes
  a close icon (×).
- Picking a scene shouldn't close the list: the visitor closes it on
  purpose.
- A picked scene should keep looping. A new option at the top cycles
  through all the scenes.
- It should work well on phones (story 1.2's request).

## UX usability audit of story 1.2's picker

| # | Finding | Heuristic | Severity | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Icon-only options are ambiguous: Dawn and Sunset differ by a small arrow; "wind" is abstract | Recognition over recall (Nielsen #6) | High | Icon plus name in every option |
| 2 | The closed button shows the current scene, not that it opens a menu | Signifiers (Norman) | Medium | Menu icon; × while open |
| 3 | The list closes after each pick, which slows down exploring | User control and freedom (#3) | Medium | Stays open; closes with × or Escape |
| 4 | A pick doesn't hold: the next crossing moves the scene on | Match with expectations (#2) | High | A pick loops; "All scenes" brings back the journey |
| 5 | The 4 s lock after a pick blocks moving between scenes | Flexibility (#7), user control | Medium | Removed; a new pick eases from wherever the scene is |
| 6 | White labels on light glass would fail contrast over a bright sky | WCAG 1.4.3 | High | Darker glass (62%); the selected option is a white pill with dark text |
| 7 | Seven labelled options are taller than a phone on its side | Mobile layout | Medium | Two-column grid to the left on short screens |
| 8 | A reload would move a picked scene on (the shuffle advances) | Consistency (#4) | Low | The pick is kept for the tab; "All scenes" clears it |

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Closed:** a 44 px glass menu button above the speaker, labelled
   "Choose the time of day". **Open:** the icon becomes ×, labelled
   "Close…", with `aria-expanded`.
2. **Options:** "All scenes", Dawn, Day, Windy, Rain, Sunset, Night. Each
   is a glass pill with an icon and its name. White labels keep at least
   4.5:1 over the brightest sky behind the list. The selected option is a
   white pill with dark text (`aria-pressed`).
3. **Picking a scene** eases to it and loops it: crossings no longer move
   it on, and a reload stays on it. **"All scenes"** restarts the journey
   from the current scene.
4. **Closing:** the list stays open after a pick or a tap elsewhere. It
   closes only with × (or Escape on a keyboard).
5. **States:** hover, press, keyboard focus, selected, and disabled (the
   style is ready for options that are unavailable).
6. **Mobile:**
   - all targets are 40–44 px tall;
   - in portrait, a column above the button, on screen;
   - on a phone on its side (520 px tall or less), a two-column grid to
     the left.
7. Tests and the build pass.
