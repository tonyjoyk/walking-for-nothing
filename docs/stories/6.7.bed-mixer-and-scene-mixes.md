# Story 6.7: Bed mixer and scene mixes

## Status

Review: built locally 2026-10-20 per party mode `docs/bmad/party-mode/2026-10-20-sound-mix.md` (D-6.7 to D-6.12); waiting for Tony's listening feedback. Not pushed (D-6.12: credits first).

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** each scene to have its own soundscape, blending into the next,
**so that** the sound follows the day and the weather.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `AUDIO_MIX` in config gives every scene, overcast included, its bed weights.
2. Beds loop sample-accurately using the manifest's loop points.
3. On a scene change, beds crossfade with equal power over the transition time (4 s by default), with no gap, dip or click.
4. Rain beds follow the eased `P.rain`; wind beds follow `0.2 + 0.8 × P.wind`.
5. Inactive beds stop about 2 seconds after reaching silence.
