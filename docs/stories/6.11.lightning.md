# Story 6.11: Lightning before thunder (P1)

## Status

Draft

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** a soft flash in the sky before the thunder,
**so that** the storm feels real, with the gap between light and sound.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each roll can be preceded by a soft, short brightening of the sky (no strobe), with the roll arriving distance ÷ 0.343 seconds later.
2. Reduced motion makes the flash much gentler.
3. It can be switched off in config.

## Tasks / subtasks

- [ ] `scene.flash(strength)`
- [ ] Wire `onFlash` from `ThunderDirector`

## Dev notes

- **Owner:** Claude.
- **Spec:** `docs/product/specs/audio/` (brief, PRD, architecture); decisions D-6.1 to D-6.5.
- Keep it subtle: the scene is calm.
- Cross-epic rule (`docs/ux/ux-spec.md`): flashes are the only exception to "nothing snaps".
  - A soft sky brightening only, never full-screen white.
  - Rise and fall each take at least 0.15 s.
  - At most one flash per roll, and never more than 3 in any second (WCAG 2.3.1).
  - Gentler with reduced motion, and possible to switch off.
- The flash is drawn in the sky and lighting layers, which are Epic 4 code.
