# Story 6.11: Lightning before thunder (P1)

## Status

Done (Lightning chat), 15 Nov 2026. Decision [D-6.16](../architecture/decisions.md#d-616-lightning-before-thunder).

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** a soft flash in the sky before the thunder,
**so that** the storm feels real, with the gap between light and sound.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each roll can be preceded by a soft, short brightening of the sky (no strobe), with the roll arriving distance ÷ 0.343 seconds later.
2. Reduced motion makes the flash much gentler.
3. It can be switched off in config.

## Tasks / subtasks

- [x] `scene.flash(strength)`
- [x] Wire `onFlash` from `ThunderDirector`

## Dev notes

- **Owner:** Claude.
- **Spec:** `docs/product/specs/audio/` (brief, PRD, architecture); decisions D-6.1 to D-6.5.
- Keep it subtle: the scene is calm.
- Cross-epic rule (`docs/ux/ux-spec.md`): flashes are the only exception to "nothing snaps".
  - A soft sky brightening only, never full-screen white.
  - Rise and fall each take at least 0.15 s.
  - At most one flash per roll, and never more than 3 in any second (WCAG 2.3.1).
  - Gentler with reduced motion, and possible to switch off.
- The flash is drawn in the sky and lighting layers, which are Epic 4 code.

## Dev agent record

- **`src/audio/thunder.js`:** `ThunderScheduler.strike()` gives a flash now
  (`{ kind: 'flash', strength, km }`) and queues the roll `km / 0.343` s
  later. The next strike counts from when the roll lands, so rolls stay at
  least 25 s apart and never overlap. A roll already on its way still lands
  if the scene leaves Rain. `thunderShape()` gains `flash` (1 at 3 km, 0.35
  at 12 km). Without lightning, behaviour is as in story 6.9.
- **`src/audio/index.js`:** the scheduler now runs every frame, with sound on
  or off (`stepStorm`). Flashes go to `onFlash`; rolls play only while sound
  is on. "Thunder now" also flashes first.
- **`src/scene/Scene.js`, `src/scene/sky.js`:** `scene.flash(strength, x)`
  and `flashLevel()`. The flash rises over 0.18 s and falls over 0.9 s
  (smoothstep), at most one per second, in real time. `drawFlash` screens a
  cool white over the sky and clouds only, fading to nothing at the horizon,
  plus a soft glow where the strike is. It's drawn before the hills, so they
  stay dark against it. Reduced motion: 30% strength, rise 0.45 s, fall 1.6 s.
- **Config:** `audio.lightning` (default true), plus a dev panel toggle.
- **`src/main.js`:** one line, `audio.onFlash = (s) => scene.flash(s)`.
