# Story 5.2: A preview image when the link is shared

## Status

Done

## Story

**As** the owner,
**I want** a good preview image to show up when I share the site's address,
**so that** people see the scene before they open it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Open Graph and Twitter card tags: `og:type`, `og:url`, `og:title`,
   `og:description`, and `og:image` with an absolute URL, its width, height
   and alt text, plus `twitter:card` = `summary_large_image`.
2. The image is 1200 × 630 (the common size for WhatsApp, iMessage,
   LinkedIn, Slack and X) and under 300 KB, so WhatsApp shows it.
3. The image is rendered from the real site, with no interface (sound
   button, bubble) and no text. Each platform shows the title and
   description under it.
4. It's served from the site root after deploy.

## Design (UX)

- **Sunset, the man alone, mid-stride at about 40% of the width,** with the
  hills and the low sun to his right. It's the warmest, most recognisable
  frame, and it reads well as a small thumbnail.
- Juhu only comes out at dawn and in daylight (the scene rules), so she
  isn't in the sunset image. That keeps the preview faithful to what
  visitors see.
- **Alternatives** in `docs/media/share/`: daylight with Juhu, and sunset
  with Juhu called in, which visitors would never see.
