# Story 6.17: Mellow thunder in Rain, with dev-panel controls

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor who keeps the site open while working,
**I want** thunder in the Rain scene to be rare, distant and soft,
**so that** it adds to the rain without startling me or repeating constantly.

**As the** owner,
**I want** the thunder's timing, distance, close roll and flash strength in
the dev panel,
**so that** I can play with the effect live.

Owner's request: "a mellow thunder that comes if somebody is using the
website while they're working. It should not happen continuously. The
thunder sound should not be too loud for them. Add this to our dev
controls because I want to play around with the thunder effect."
Decision: [D-6.18](../architecture/decisions.md#d-618-mellow-thunder-with-dev-controls)
(supersedes the thunder timing of story [6.9](6.9.thunder.md)).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The first strike comes at least 30 s after rain arrives
   (`firstAfter: [30, 50]`).
2. Rolls are at least 60 s apart, about 100 s on average (roughly every
   1–2.5 minutes), and never overlap.
3. Rolls are mostly far: 6–12 km by default.
4. The loud close roll is off by default (`audio.thunderClose: false`); when
   switched on, it plays at most once per visit, and only nearer than 5 km.
5. The default thunder volume is 0.55 (was 0.9), under the rain bed.
6. Lightning still flashes first and the roll still lands distance ÷ 0.343 s
   later; "Thunder now" flashes too.
7. The dev panel's Sound folder has: thunder on/off, thunder volume, how
   often (s), min gap (s), nearest (km), farthest (km), close roll on/off,
   lightning on/off, flash strength (0–1) and "Thunder now".
8. Every new value has a range in `RANGES`, is validated and clamped on
   load, and saves and loads like the others.
9. Changes in the panel take effect without a reload.
10. Tests and the build pass.

## Dev notes

- `AUDIO_THUNDER.close` is the close roll's file id, so the default switch
  is `AUDIO_THUNDER.closeRoll`; the live switch is `audio.thunderClose`.
- Ranges: how often 20–600 s, min gap 10–300 s, distances 1–20 km, flash
  strength 0–1. The close roll needs nearest under 5 km to be heard.
- Without lightning, a strike is the roll itself; the first roll still
  comes ≥ 30 s in.
