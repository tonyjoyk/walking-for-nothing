## Story

**As a** Tony,
**I want** the shortlisted recordings auditioned and the best take chosen for each layer,
**so that** every scene is built from recordings that actually sound right, with no traffic, voices or music.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each layer in the brief's shortlist has a primary and a backup pick.
2. Each pick records the time range to use, plus anything to cut (traffic, voices, temple music).
3. `audio/sources.json` lists every pick with source URL, recordist, licence, trims and the scenes it belongs to.
4. The PRD's open question 1 is answered (Kerala-authentic set, which includes CC BY-NC, or open-only set) and noted in the change log.
