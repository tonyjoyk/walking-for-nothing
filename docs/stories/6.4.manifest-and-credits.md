# Story 6.4: Manifest and credits

## Status

Draft

## Story

**As a** visitor and owner,
**I want** every shipped sound traceable and credited,
**so that** the licences are honoured (D-6.2) and recordists are credited.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `public/audio/manifest.json` lists each file: role, scenes, duration, loop points in samples, and credit (title, recordist, URL, licence, edited note).
2. Files derived from BY-SA sources are labelled CC BY-SA 4.0.
3. A test fails on any licence outside the allowlist, or any missing credit field.
4. The build regenerates the README's "Sound credits" block from the manifest.
