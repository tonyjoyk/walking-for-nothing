# Story 2.1: Pause on hover; turn on a 2 s rest or a click

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** the man to stop when my cursor is on him, and to turn to me only
if I stay or click him,
**so that** the moment feels like a person noticing me, never a glitch.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given he is walking, when the cursor comes onto him, then he starts
   slowing by the next frame. He comes to rest with both feet down and
   stands, without turning.
2. Given the cursor stays on him, when 2 s (`greeting.dwellSeconds`) have
   passed since it arrived, then he turns (±0.05 s, or as soon as he's
   standing if the stop takes longer). Then he looks, smiles, turns back
   and walks on as before.
3. Given he is walking, stopping or standing, when the visitor clicks on
   him, then he turns as soon as he's standing, with no wait. The greeting
   plays through even if the cursor leaves.
4. Given a clicked greeting, when the visitor clicks elsewhere, then it
   ends, as a tap elsewhere does.
5. Given he hasn't turned yet, when the cursor leaves, then he walks on
   within 2.5 s. If the cursor returns, he stops again with no cooldown.
6. Given a still cursor, he can't walk out from under it while stopping,
   and walking into a still cursor stops him. Hover is re-tested every
   frame.
7. Unchanged: no snapping (largest per-frame change < 0.04), feet planted
   while standing and when walking on, the cooldown and "come back again"
   rule after he has turned, touch taps, "Greet now", no NaN.
8. `dwellSeconds` is in config (0–6 s, validated) and in the dev panel.

### Completion notes

- **New state:** `waiting` (standing, not turned). `hoverT` is the dwell
  clock, and resets the moment the cursor leaves.
- **The cooldown and `rearm`** apply only if he turned (`turned`).
- **Re-stop from `starting`:** the speed curve is scaled by the entry speed
  (`stopFrom`), so there's no jump.
- **Stop timing:** the stop is planned in the frame the hover begins, so he
  slows from the next frame (it was one frame later).
- **Hysteresis:** `STOP_REACH` = 0.65 of a stride behind him while greeting.
- **Clicks:** `pointerdown` handles mouse and touch alike. Clicks on buttons
  or the dev panel are ignored.
