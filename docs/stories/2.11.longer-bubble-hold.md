# Story 2.11: The bubble holds 2 s longer

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** the speech bubble to stay a little longer,
**so that** I have time to read it before he walks on.

## Acceptance criteria

1. After its reveal, the bubble holds 6 s instead of 4 s (`readSeconds` 6),
   so it's about 7 s on screen in all.
2. The hold still pauses while the cursor is on the bubble, and a click on
   him still dismisses it.
3. Tests and the build pass.
