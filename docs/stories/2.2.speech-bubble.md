# Story 2.2: A speech bubble when he faces you

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor who has found the greeting,
**I want** the man to say a few words about who he is,
**so that** I get a sense of the person behind the site.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given he turns to face the visitor (after a 2 s rest, a click or a tap),
   when the turn completes, then a speech bubble with the agreed text fades
   in over 0.8 s.
2. The bubble sits above his head, with a tail pointing to it. It stays
   inside the viewport with a 16 px margin, at desktop and phone sizes.
3. While the bubble is on, he faces the visitor for at least `readSeconds`
   (15 s) in all before turning back on his own.
4. With the cursor on the bubble, he keeps facing the visitor. Leaving both
   the bubble and him ends the greeting as usual.
5. When he starts turning back, the bubble fades out over 0.8 s. It never
   shows while he walks.
6. Style matches the site:
   - system font, a light translucent surface with blur, 14 px corners, a
     soft shadow;
   - no border, links, icons or close button;
   - readable in every scene, day and night.
7. With `greeting.bubble` off, nothing shows and the timing is the original
   3 s + 4 s. `bubble` and `readSeconds` are validated settings in the dev
   panel.
8. The text is real HTML: it's selectable, and screen readers can read it
   when it appears.

### Completion notes

- **The tail** is a clipped triangle below the bubble, not a rotated
  square. A square showed through the translucent surface as a diamond.
- **The bubble as hover:** the cursor on the bubble sets `hovering`
  directly, so he stays facing. The next move off it goes back to the
  normal per-frame test.
- **Clicks on the bubble** are ignored, so selecting text doesn't end the
  greeting.
