# Story 6.19: The speaker shows off until sound plays, with a "Click for sound" cue

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** the speaker to show the real state of the sound, and a small hint
that I can turn it on,
**so that** I'm not told sound is on while the page is silent, and I find it
without being lectured.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Off on load:** the speaker shows off (`aria-pressed="false"`, "Turn
   sound on") until sound actually plays.
2. **Any interaction starts it:** the first click, tap or key anywhere,
   including on the man or on the cue, starts the sound, and the speaker
   switches to on. A click on the speaker also starts it; later clicks
   toggle it.
3. **Remembered off:** after a visitor turns sound off, later visits stay
   silent, clicks on the page don't start it, and the cue doesn't show.
4. **The cue,** on every visit while sound is off:
   - a small dark-glass pill beside the speaker with an arrow, "Click for
     sound" (mouse) or "Tap for sound" (touch);
   - two soft rings from the speaker;
   - in at about 1.5 s, out about 5 s later, or at once when sound starts
     or the controls are used;
   - no close button; `role="status"`;
   - reduced motion: no rings, a steady soft glow.
5. It fits beside the controls on a phone, upright and on its side.
6. Tests and the build pass.

## QA results

**Gate: PASS** (15 Nov 2026, QA (Claude)). Headless Chrome with
`--autoplay-policy=user-gesture-required` and `--mute-audio`, a fresh
profile per case, real mouse and touch input:

| Case | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Desktop, no interaction | Speaker off, no audio context. Cue hidden at 0.6 s, shown at 2.1 s ("Click for sound", rings on), gone by 7.1 s |
| Click on the sky, after the cue | Audio context running; speaker on |
| Click on the sky while the cue shows | Sound starts; cue gone at once |
| Click on the man | Sound starts; he turns to greet as before |
| Speaker click | Sound starts, speaker on; a second click turns it off (`wfn.sound=off`) |
| Reload after "off", then click the sky | No cue; no audio context; stays silent |
| Phone 390×844 and 844×390 | "Tap for sound" fits left of the speaker; a tap starts the sound |
| Reduced motion | No ring animation; a steady soft glow |
