# Story 2.3: A smaller, more minimal speech bubble

## Status

Done

## Story

**As** the owner,
**I want** the speech bubble smaller and quieter,
**so that** it introduces me without taking over the scene.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The bubble uses the option the owner picked from
   [the four options](../ux/research/speech-bubble-options.md): **Air**.
2. Type is smaller than the options' 12 px (owner: "a bit smaller"): 11 px,
   188 px wide.
3. The same style in every scene. The text reaches at least 4.5:1 against
   the sky behind it in all six scenes and at phone width, and never covers
   the man. The ink adapts, with a veil only where it's needed (D-2.4).
4. The other options and the `?bubble=` switch are removed.
5. The copy is: "Hey, I'm Tony, a product designer from Kerala, India." /
   "I lead design at Resolve to Save Lives, a public health nonprofit, where
   I design tools and services for healthcare staff." / "The internet often
   feels rushed and demanding. So I created a digital space where we can
   slow down and feel calm."
6. All tests and the build pass. Behaviour and timing are unchanged from
   2.2.

### Completion notes

- **The ink choice:** `chooseInk(px, current)` in `bubble.js` is a pure
  function, so it's unit-tested. The canvas is read through a 24×24
  downscale about every 0.4 s, and only while the bubble shows.
- **The veil:** a blurred `::before`, 20 px larger than the text, so its
  soft edge falls outside the words. Its strength is computed for each
  sampled pixel by blending in sRGB, then judged on the worst tenth.
- **The Miro board** keeps all four options as a record:
  https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVEXknK0w=/
