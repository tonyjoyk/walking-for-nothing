# Story 6.15: Sound on by default, with a first-visit hint

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** first-time visitor,
**I want** the walk to have its sound from the start, and a quiet pointer to
the sound and time-of-day controls,
**so that** I get the full experience and know I can change it, or close
the pointer and simply enjoy it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Sound is on by default.** It starts on the first click, tap or key
   anywhere (browsers allow nothing earlier). The speaker shows "on".
2. **A first click on the speaker starts the sound**; it doesn't turn it
   off. Later clicks toggle it as usual.
3. **Turning sound off is remembered:** the next visit stays silent, and
   clicks on the page don't start it.
4. **The hint** shows once per visitor, about 1.5 s after load, beside the
   controls with an arrow:
   - "Sound is on"
   - "It starts with your first click. Set the volume or time of day
     here." Once sound plays, the middle sentence drops.
5. **It closes** by itself after 6 s (the countdown pauses while it's
   hovered or focused), with ×, or as soon as the controls are used. If
   the controls are used before it appears, it never shows.
6. **Accessibility:**
   - polite status (`role="status"`);
   - a labelled close button with a focus ring;
   - white text on 72% dark glass;
   - a fade only with reduced motion.
7. **Mobile:** fits beside the controls in portrait and on its side, and a
   tap starts the sound.
8. Tests and the build pass.
