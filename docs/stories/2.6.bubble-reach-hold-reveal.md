# Story 2.6: Reach the bubble, hold on touch, reveal the text

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** to move to the bubble (or tap near it) without it vanishing, to
have enough time to read it and tap the link, and to see the words appear
gently,
**so that** the introduction feels calm and the link is actually usable.

## Acceptance criteria

1. While the bubble shows, the cursor anywhere in the box around him and
   the bubble (plus 12 px) keeps him facing the visitor, including the gap
   between his head and the bubble. Outside that box, the greeting ends as
   before.
2. On touch, a tap inside that box doesn't dismiss the bubble; a tap
   outside it does.
3. The bubble holds for `readSeconds` (4 s) after the reveal completes,
   about 5.1 s in all, then he turns back. With the cursor on the bubble
   itself, the 4 s is paused. While he faces the visitor, a click or tap on
   him dismisses it at once. (Changed from 15 s by the owner mid-story.)
4. The four lines are revealed in order: plate 0.4 s, each line a 4 px rise
   and fade over 0.5 s, staggered 0.15 s, complete at about 1.1 s. With
   reduced motion, a single fade with no rise or stagger.
5. Fade-out, look, copy, link, plate and contrast are unchanged.
6. Tests and the build pass.
