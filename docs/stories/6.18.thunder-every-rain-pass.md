# Story 6.18: Lightning and thunder in every Rain pass

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor watching the Rain scene,
**I want** to see lightning and hear its thunder every time it rains,
**so that** the storm feels alive rather than something I'd rarely catch.

## Owner feedback (15 Nov 2026)

- Story 6.17's timing was far too rare: the first strike came 30–50 s
  after Rain started, but a Rain pass is only about 16–33 s, so most
  visitors never saw it.
- **Wanted:**
  - lightning and thunder should not be rare;
  - the thunder should come as soon as the lightning;
  - in Rain, every pass across the screen should have at least one
    lightning flash and one thunder roll.
- **Scope:** Rain only (the owner's choice; the other scenes stay
  clear-sky).

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The first strike** comes 3–8 s after Rain arrives.
2. **Strikes are 8–13 s apart** (13 s is a hard maximum), so any 16 s of
   Rain, the shortest pass on a phone, has at least one.
3. **Thunder lands 0.4–1.2 s after its flash.** Every flash is clearly
   visible.
4. **Still gentle:** the soft roll at volume 0.55, with no loud close roll
   unless it's switched on.
5. **Dev panel:** a "max gap (s)" control. "How often", "min gap" and the
   distances can be set to these values; all new settings are validated
   and saved.
6. Tests and the build pass.

## QA results

**Gate: PASS** (15 Nov 2026, QA (Claude)).

- **Unit test, 40 random runs of 10 minutes:**
  - first flash at 3–8 s;
  - every roll within 1.3 s of its flash;
  - gaps of 8–14.3 s;
  - every 16 s window has a strike;
  - only the soft roll.
- **Real browser, Rain, sound on after one click:**

  | Time | Event |
  | --- | --- |
  | 2.7 s | flash |
  | 3.7 s | roll |
  | 16.7 s | flash |
  | 17.3 s | roll |
  | about 28 s | scene moved on to Sunset |

  Two strikes in one pass.
- **Suite:** 112 tests pass; the build passes.
