## How to listen

### Setup

1. `npm run dev` in the repo. Vite serves on `http://localhost:5173/`.
2. Open a scene with the dev panel:

   | Scene (picker label) | URL |
   | --- | --- |
   | Dawn | `http://localhost:5173/?weather=dawn&dev` |
   | Day | `http://localhost:5173/?weather=sunny&dev` |
   | Windy | `http://localhost:5173/?weather=wind&dev` |
   | Rain | `http://localhost:5173/?weather=rain&dev` |
   | Sunset | `http://localhost:5173/?weather=twilight&dev` |
   | Night | `http://localhost:5173/?weather=night&dev` |
   | Overcast (optional, no picker option) | `http://localhost:5173/?weather=overcast&dev` |

3. **Click once anywhere** on the page. Sound is on by default but starts
   only on the first click, tap or key (story 6.15).
4. **Hold the scene.** `?weather=` only sets the opening scene; the
   journey still moves on after each crossing. To stay put, pick the same
   scene in the visitor menu (the menu button above the speaker): a
   picked scene loops (story 1.3). "All scenes" brings the journey back.
5. Open the dev panel's **Sound** folder. Its **playing** readout lists
   the beds and their levels, and the last bird or thunder event. Keep it
   visible while you listen.

Note: don't change scenes with the dev panel's **scene** dropdown (first
folder). It changes the look only; the sound stays on the old scene. Use
the visitor menu or the URL.

### Per scene (about 2 minutes each)

1. **First 30 s: hands off.** Just listen at the default volume (60%).
2. **Next 60 s: listen for the events.** Note how often birds come and
   whether any call stands out. Press **Bird now** a few times to hear the
   variants back to back.
3. **Last 30 s: play with it.**
   - **Volume slider** (hover the speaker, bottom right): try 100%, 30%
     and 10%. Is the balance the same at every level?
   - **Layer toggles** (wind layer, rain layer, bird layer): switch each
     off and on to hear what it adds.
   - In Rain only: **Thunder now** (after 6.17 is merged).
4. Fill in the scene's row of the scorecard.

Do all six on **headphones** first (detail, loop seams, clicks), then all
six again on **laptop speakers** (balance, what disappears). Two passes of
about 15 minutes each. Write values you land on in the notes column: the
dev panel doesn't save them (only the visitor volume is remembered), but
**Copy JSON** in "Save and share" copies everything.

## What you should hear

Levels are what the **playing** readout shows: a bed's weight from
`AUDIO_MIX`, times how windy or rainy the scene is for the wind and rain
beds. All beds then go through `audio.beds` (90%) and the master (60%).
Birds are spread across left and right, near or far (far is quieter and
duller).

| Scene | Beds | Events (from `AUDIO_EVENTS`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dawn** | Orchard dawn chorus, full (`dawnOrchard` 100%); a faint breeze under it (`windGrass` about 11%). | A bird about every 16 s (never closer than 6 s), so 7 or 8 in 2 minutes. Two in three are a koel: 2–4 calls rising slightly. The rest a kingfisher: 1–2 calls. |
| **Day** | Daytime village field, crickets and distant birds (`dayField` 100%); a light breeze (`windGrass` about 15%). | A bird about every 14 s (6 s minimum), 8 or 9 in 2 minutes. Three in four are a koel, the rest a kingfisher. |
| **Windy** | Wind in the grass, strong (`windGrass` about 56%); the day field faint underneath (`dayField` 30%). | No birds. (Bird now still plays a Day bird: that's the test button, not what visitors hear.) |
| **Rain** | Steady rain on banana leaves (`rainSteady` 100%). No wind bed, though the scene looks windy. | No birds. Thunder: a lightning flash, then the roll a few seconds later (further strikes: longer gap, quieter, duller). First strike soon after the rain arrives, then roughly one every 40 s or more; one close roll per visit at most. Birds duck under thunder. Exact timing: story 6.17. |
| **Sunset** | One bed: evening birds giving way to crickets (`eveningBirdsToCrickets` 100%). | House crows, rare: about every 90 s (40 s minimum), so 0–2 in 2 minutes. Use Bird now to hear them. |
| **Night** | Crickets on a quiet road (`nightCrickets` 100%). Its loop is short, 45 s, so it repeats nearly three times in 2 minutes. | None. |
| Overcast (optional) | Day field, softer (`dayField` 55%); some breeze (`windGrass` about 24%). | A koel about every 30 s (10 s minimum). |

Every bed except Night loops after 75 s. The PRD also lists sounds that
aren't in the set yet: bulbul, barbet, myna, coucal, owlet, nightjar,
frogs at night and in the rain. If you miss one, write it in "missing?":
it needs sourcing (a new story), not a config change.

## Cross-scene checks

| # | Check | How | What's right | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Crossfades** | Choose "All scenes" in the visitor menu and let the journey run through a few scenes. Also jump between scenes in the menu, including Rain → Day and Night → Dawn. | One soundscape blends into the next over about 8 s (`audio.crossfadeSeconds`; the picture takes 4 s). No click, gap, dip or bump. Rain fades with the rain on screen. | |
| 2 | **First-click start** | Fresh tab (or private window), any scene URL, don't touch anything for 5 s, then click once on the sky. Repeat with a key press, and with the speaker as the first click. | Silence until the click, then sound within about 2 s, fading in, not jumping in. The speaker shows "on". A first click on the speaker starts sound, doesn't turn it off. | |
| 3 | **Volume slider at low levels** | Set 10%, 5%, 2%, then 0. | Still the same balance at 5–10%: birds don't vanish before the beds, or stick out above them. At 0 the speaker shows muted. Raising it again brings sound back smoothly. | |
| 4 | **Fading when the window loses focus** (story 6.16) | With sound playing, click another app so it's in front while the page is still visible; wait 3 s; click back. Then switch to another tab and back. | Another app: sound fades out within about 2 s, the scene keeps moving; it fades back in on return. Another tab: scene and sound pause, then resume. Volume and on/off choices unchanged. | |
| 5 | **Thunder and lightning in Rain** (after story 6.17) | Rain, held, 3–4 minutes. Press Thunder now a few times, at least 30 s apart. Then leave Rain mid-roll. | Flash first, then the roll; the gap grows with distance. Never two rolls at once. Peak no more than about 6 dB over the rain (PRD P0.4). A roll already on its way still finishes after you leave Rain; no new ones start. Values: as set by 6.17. | |
| 6 | **No pattern (AC 2)** | One 15-minute session on "All scenes", headphones, not looking at the screen. | No call, loop seam or thunder roll you start to recognise as "that one again". Listen especially at Night (45 s loop) and Day (most birds). | |

## How notes turn into changes

All in `src/config.js` unless noted. Change one value at a time, and try
it first with the dev panel control where there is one.

| Note | Change | Dev panel control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Birds too busy at dawn" (or too sparse) | `AUDIO_EVENTS.dawn.mean` up (sparser) or down, and `minGap`. Per scene: `AUDIO_EVENTS.<scene>`. | bird density (`audio.eventDensity`: scales every scene at once, so use it to find the right feel, then set the scene's `mean`) |
| "Birds too busy everywhere" | `audio.eventDensity` default (1) in `createDefaultConfig()` | bird density |
| "Birds too loud" or "too quiet" | `audio.birds` (0.7) | bird volume |
| "Too much koel, not enough kingfisher" | `AUDIO_EVENTS.<scene>.pool` weights | — |
| "Koel phrase too long" or "calls too close" | `AUDIO_CALLS.koel.phrase` (calls per event) and `gap` (seconds between); `rise` for the climb | — |
| "Breeze too loud at dawn" (or any bed in one scene) | That bed's weight in `AUDIO_MIX.<scene>`, e.g. `AUDIO_MIX.dawn.windGrass` | wind volume / rain volume to try it (they change every scene) |
| "Windy isn't windy enough" | `AUDIO_MIX.wind.windGrass` up, `AUDIO_MIX.wind.dayField` down | wind volume |
| "Rain needs some wind" | add `windGrass` to `AUDIO_MIX.rain` | — |
| "All beds too loud against the birds" | `audio.beds` (0.9) | beds volume |
| "Wind or rain too loud everywhere" | `audio.wind` / `audio.rain` (1) | wind volume / rain volume |
| "Whole site too loud or quiet by default" | `audio.master` (0.6) | master, or the visitor slider |
| "Crossfade too slow" or "too abrupt" | `audio.crossfadeSeconds` (8) | crossfade (s) |
| "Thunder too often, too loud, too close, flash gap wrong" | **Story 6.17's area** (`AUDIO_THUNDER`, `audio.thunder`, `src/audio/thunder.js`). Pass the note to that chat, or wait until 6.17 is Done. | thunder volume, Thunder now |
| "I can hear the loop" or "a click at the seam" | Not config: the loop points and length in `audio/sources.json`, rebuilt with `scripts/build-audio.mjs` (story 6.3) | — |
| "Sounds wrong for Kerala" or "missing a sound" | A new recording: `audio/sources.json` and a new story (sourcing as in 6.1 and 6.2) | — |
| "Overcast isn't right" | `AUDIO_MIX.overcast`, `AUDIO_EVENTS.overcast` | — |

Don't change the scene presets' `wind` and `rain` values to fix sound:
they also drive the picture (the wind and rain beds follow them).
