# Story 6.12: Sound credits on the page

## Status

Draft

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** to see who recorded the sounds,
**so that** recordists are credited as their licences require.

## Acceptance criteria

1. One quiet "Credits" link sits at the bottom left (D-0.5), and there's never more than one credit link on screen.
2. It opens one list built from the manifest (title, recordist, link and licence for every CC BY, BY-SA and BY-NC file), plus the Open-Meteo credit whenever live weather is on.
3. The existing standalone `#credit` "Weather: Open-Meteo" link (in `index.html` and `main.js`) moves into this shared list and is removed as a separate link.
4. The list is keyboard and screen-reader accessible.
