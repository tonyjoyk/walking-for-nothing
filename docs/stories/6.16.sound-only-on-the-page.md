# Story 6.16: Sound only while the visitor is on the page

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** the site to go quiet when I'm not on it,
**so that** it never plays sound while I'm doing something else.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Switching to another tab, or minimising the window, pauses the scene
   and the sound. Coming back resumes both. (This already worked; checked
   again.)
2. With another app or window in front, and the page still visible, the
   sound fades out within about 2 s while the scene keeps moving.
   Returning fades it back in.
3. The visitor's own sound and volume choices are unchanged.
4. Tests and the build pass.

## Tasks

- [x] `main.js`: `syncAway()` on window `blur`/`focus` and

## QA results

**Gate: PASS** (15 Nov 2026, QA (Claude)). Headless Chrome after sound
started:

| Situation | Page hidden | Audio | Master gain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| On the page | no | running | 0.60 |
| Another tab in front | yes | suspended | (scene paused) |
| Another app in front, 1 s | no | running | 0.13 (fading) |
| Another app in front, 2.5 s | no | running | 0.01 (silent); the scene still moves |
| Back on the page, 1 s | no | running | 0.48 (fading in) |
| Back on the page, 2.5 s | no | running | 0.59 |

The "another app" case was simulated with the window's real `blur`/`focus`
events, since headless Chrome has no OS window focus. 106 tests pass; the
build passes.
