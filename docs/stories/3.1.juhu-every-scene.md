# Story 3.1: Juhu in the first scene and in every scene

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** to meet Juhu early, whatever the scene,
**so that** the walk feels like the two of them from the start.

## Acceptance criteria

1. She can appear in all six scenes, and her colours match each scene's
   light.
2. **First visit:** no earlier than 6 s after load, and once he's past the
   middle of the screen. She trots in from the left and joins him within
   the first scene.
3. She walks on with him into the second scene and leaves once he's past
   the middle of it. A scene change alone never sends her away.
4. Then he walks alone for two scenes, and she returns in the fifth. The
   pattern repeats: scenes 5–6 together, 7–8 alone, and so on.
5. The new settings are in the dev panel: "first visit (s)" and "first
   visit from (width)". The other dog settings still work. "Call Juhu now"
   still works.
6. Tests and the build pass.
