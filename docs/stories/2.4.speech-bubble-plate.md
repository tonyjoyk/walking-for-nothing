# Story 2.4: A plain 40% white plate behind the bubble

## Status

Done

## Story

**As** the owner,
**I want** the bubble's words on one simple, consistent background,
**so that** it looks the same and reads clearly in every scene, without the
blurry smudge on Sunset and Rain.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The blurred veil and the light/dark ink switching are gone.
2. In every scene, dark text sits on a white rectangle with 8 px corners at
   40% opacity.
3. Text contrast is at least 4.5:1 in all six scenes and on phones. If 40%
   would fall below that, the plate becomes 60%.
4. Size, copy, hairline, placement, timing and behaviour are unchanged.
5. All tests and the build pass.
