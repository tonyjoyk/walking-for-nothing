# Story 6.14: A volume slider for visitors

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor working with the site open (say, in a meeting),
**I want** to turn the site's sound down without touching my laptop's
volume,
**so that** the site can stay quiet while everything else stays loud.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A slim glass pill with a vertical volume slider rises above the sound
   button:
   - on hover, with a mouse or trackpad;
   - on keyboard focus;
   - for about 3.5 s after a tap on touch screens.

   It hides again when the pointer leaves or focus moves away. It doesn't
   stay open because of focus left behind by a click, or touch's "sticky"
   hover.
2. The speaker still mutes and unmutes on click or tap.
3. The slider sets the site's volume from 0 to 100% (`config.audio.master`,
   default 60%). Arrow keys change it.
   - It's remembered in this browser.
   - Raising it while sound is off turns sound on.
   - At 0, the speaker shows the muted icon.
4. **States:**
   - hover (full opacity);
   - press (darker glass, scale 0.96);
   - keyboard focus (white ring);
   - disabled (35% opacity, no pointer) when the browser can't play audio.

   The look matches the existing glass button.
5. Storage that's missing or blocked never throws. The dev panel's master,
   mute and sound-on controls keep working.
6. Tests and the build pass.

**Fixed during QA:**
- The slider stayed open after a click (`:focus-within`), so it now uses
  `:has(:focus-visible)`.
- It stayed open after a tap (sticky hover), so hover now applies only for
  `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- The upright slider sat off-centre in its pill, so it's now centred
  before rotating.

**Not tested by machine:** the disabled state needs a browser without Web
Audio. The CSS and the `disable()` path are reviewed.
