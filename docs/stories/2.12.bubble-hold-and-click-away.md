# Story 2.12: The bubble holds 9 s from its reveal; a click elsewhere ends it

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** the speech bubble to stay long enough to read, for a predictable
time, and to end when I click or tap elsewhere,
**so that** I can read it and move on when I choose.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The bubble holds 9 s from the moment it's fully revealed (`readSeconds`
   9), whether or not the cursor is on him. Then he turns back and walks
   on, even with the cursor still on him.
2. Only the cursor on the bubble itself pauses the hold (to reach the link).
3. Once he has turned, moving the cursor off him without clicking doesn't
   end the greeting early; the same hold carries on.
4. A click or tap anywhere outside him and the bubble makes him turn back
   and walk on at once. A click on him still does too.
5. Before he turns (stopping, settling), leaving him still gets him walking
   again within 2.5 s.
6. Tests and the build pass.

## Tasks

- [x] `config.js`: `readSeconds` 9
- [x] `greeting.js`: once he turns with the bubble on, the greeting plays
