# Story 1.2: A time-of-day picker for visitors

## Status

Done

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** to choose the time of day myself,
**so that** I can stay in the scene I like (dawn, rain, night…).

## Acceptance criteria

1. A round glass button above the sound button shows the current scene's
   icon. It looks the same as the sound button (the shared `.ctl` style).
2. A click or tap opens six minimal icons: Dawn, Day, Windy, Rain, Sunset,
   Night. Each has a label (`aria-label` and a tooltip). The current one
   shows as selected (`aria-pressed`).
3. Picking one eases the scene there, and the button's icon and label
   follow. A reload then continues from it.
4. While a scene change is easing in (4 s), the six are disabled. They're
   enabled again afterwards.
5. Escape, a click or tap elsewhere, or picking a scene closes the list.
   The button shows `aria-expanded`.
6. **States:** hover, press, keyboard focus, selected and disabled.
7. **Mobile:**
   - all targets are 44 px;
   - in portrait, the six open upwards and stay on screen;
   - on a phone on its side (520 px tall or less), they open to the left as
     a row;
   - everything works by tap, and a tap elsewhere closes it.
8. The volume slider no longer opens upwards over the picker. It slides
   out to the left of the speaker, and never overlaps it.
9. Tests and the build pass.
