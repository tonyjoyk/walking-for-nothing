# Story 6.9: Thunder in the rain

## Status

Review: built locally 2026-10-20 per party mode `docs/bmad/party-mode/2026-10-20-sound-mix.md` (D-6.7 to D-6.12); waiting for Tony's listening feedback. Not pushed (D-6.12: credits first).

## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** the occasional distant roll of thunder in the rain,
**so that** the rain has character but stays calm.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Thunder plays only in the Rain scene.
2. The first roll comes at least 15 s after rain starts; after that, every 40–120 s on average (config), at least 30 s apart, never overlapping.
3. Each roll gets a random distance of 3–12 km: gain −(d − 3) dB, low-pass `lerp(3000, 600, (d − 3) / 9)` Hz, rate 0.92–1.0.
4. When the scene leaves Rain, no new rolls start; a roll already playing finishes.
5. The peak is no more than about 6 dB above the rain bed.
