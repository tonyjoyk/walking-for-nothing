## Story

**As a** visitor,
**I want** sound to load quickly and only when I turn it on,
**so that** the page stays light and sound starts within 2 seconds.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With sound off: zero audio requests (checked in headless Chrome).
2. After a tap: the manifest loads, the current scene's files are decoded first and the rest load when the browser is idle.
3. Format chosen with `canPlayType('audio/webm; codecs=opus')`, falling back to M4A.
4. A failed or blocked file marks that layer unavailable; generated sound covers it; nothing is logged as an error.
5. The first scene is audible within 2 seconds on a normal connection.
