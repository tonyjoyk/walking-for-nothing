# Party mode: how the sounds work together (20 Oct 2026)

**Question (owner):** "take a call on how best to use the sounds together,
and then go ahead and execute it on my local." The 22 processed files from
story 6.3 (6 beds, 6 thunder rolls, 10 bird calls) are in `public/audio/`.

**Roles:** Analyst, Sound designer (UX), PM, Architect, Dev, QA.
**Outcome:** decisions D-6.7 to D-6.12 (below, and in
`docs/product/specs/audio/architecture.md` §7), carried out as stories
6.5–6.10 in one pass, local only.

## Analyst: what the sound has to fit (measured)

- **A scene lasts about 35 s.** The man crosses in about 33 s
  (`walkSpeed` 70, option A), and the cycle moves on after every crossing
  (`crossingsPerScene` 1). A visit can be longer when he stops to greet.
- **The full cycle is about 3.5 minutes:** dawn → sunny → wind → rain →
  twilight → night.
- **Visual changes ease over 4 s** (`transitionSeconds`).
- **The beds are 75 s long** (night 44.6 s), so a bed usually plays
  **once, partly, per visit** and rarely reaches its loop point. The loop
  matters mostly when someone stops the cycle or greets the man for a while.
- **Some sounds are shared across scenes.** `windGrass` sits under dawn,
  sunny and wind, and `dayField` under sunny and wind. A shared bed should
  keep playing through the change, not restart.
- **The dawn bed already contains** koel, kingfisher and myna. Extra
  one-shots at dawn would double the birds.

## Sound designer (UX)

1. **One continuous place.** Moving from one scene to the next should sound
   like time passing in the same field, not a track change. So:
   - crossfade **slower than the picture**: 8 s, equal-power, so the total
     loudness never dips or bumps in the middle;
   - shared beds carry across;
   - when a bed comes back (one cycle later), **start it from a random
     point**, so the same birds don't greet every return.
2. **The bed is the room; the one-shots are characters.**
   - **Koel** is the voice of a Kerala morning, but it never calls once. It
     repeats "ku-ooo" 2–4 times, rising. Play **phrases**: 2–4 calls 1.4–2.2 s
     apart, each a touch higher and louder.
   - **Kingfisher:** a single call, sometimes two.
   - **Crows** at sunset, rarely: about one visit in three has one call.
   - Every one-shot is **placed**: panned left or right, and made softer and
     duller with distance (−1 to −9 dB, low-pass 9 → 3.5 kHz), so they sit
     in the field rather than in your ear.
3. **Where the birds go.**
   - Dawn: a few koel phrases and kingfisher calls, sparse, because the bed
     is already busy (mean 16 s).
   - Sunny: the koel is the star (mean 14 s).
   - Wind, rain and night: none. Birds go quiet in wind and rain, and
     there's no night bird in the set.
   - Twilight: crows only.
4. **Thunder belongs to the rain scene and is rare.**
   - A visit is only about 35 s, so: the first roll 6–14 s after rain
     arrives, then a mean of 40 s, never closer than 25 s apart. That's one
     roll per visit, sometimes two.
   - Distance 3–12 km: far rolls are quieter (−1 dB per km past 3), duller
     (low-pass 3 kHz → 600 Hz) and slightly slower.
   - The **close roll only when the distance is under 5 km**, and at most
     once per rain visit.
   - When a roll plays, **any bird in the scene ducks** (none are scheduled
     in rain anyway, but a bird phrase finishing during the crossfade must
     not sit on top of thunder).
5. **Level with the picture.**
   - Rain follows how hard it's raining (eased `P.rain`).
   - Wind follows how windy it is (`0.25 + 0.75 × P.wind`).
   - Keep Tony's "wind is too loud" note: the wind bed is already −8 dB at
     build, and its scene weight starts at 0.6.

## PM: requirements for this pass

- **R1:** with sound on, each scene plays its bed mix; changing scenes
  crossfades over 8 s; shared beds don't restart.
- **R2:** birds per the table above; koel in phrases; no bird in wind, rain
  or night.
- **R3:** thunder only in rain, per the timing above, with distance shaping.
- **R4:** nothing is downloaded until the visitor turns sound on. Then the
  current scene's files load first, the rest when the browser is idle.
- **R5:** while a recording is still loading (or fails), today's generated
  wind and rain fill in, and fade out once the recording arrives.
- **R6:** the existing sound button, mute, master and the dev panel keep
  working. The dev panel gains: beds, thunder, **Bird now**,
  **Thunder now**, and a readout of what's playing.
- **Out of scope for this pass:** lightning (6.11), the on-page Credits link
  (6.12) and the listening QA (6.13).
- **Release gate:** the CC BY recordings need visible credit wherever they
  play. So **this pass stays local, and isn't pushed or deployed, until 6.12
  (Credits) is done.**

## Architect

- `src/audio.js` becomes `src/audio/`, split into pure logic and the parts
  that touch the browser:

  | File | What it does | Pure / testable in node |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | `mix.js` | scene → target weight per bed; equal-power easing | yes |
  | `events.js` | the bird scheduler (seeded, Poisson, phrases, no repeat) | yes |
  | `thunder.js` | the thunder scheduler and distance → gain, filter, rate | yes |
  | `loader.js` | manifest, format choice, fetch, decode, cache | no |
  | `synth.js` | today's generated wind and rain (`SynthFallback`) | no |
  | `index.js` | `SoundDirector`: context, buses, plays what the above decide | no |

- `SoundDirector` keeps `AudioManager`'s public face (`start`, `suspend`,
  `resume`, `update`, `chirpFlock`), so `main.js` and the dev panel change
  by a line or two. `update` also takes the scene name.
- The mix table, event pools and thunder timing live in `src/config.js`
  (`AUDIO_MIX`, `AUDIO_EVENTS`, `AUDIO_THUNDER`). Volumes live in
  `config.audio` with `RANGES`.
- **Gain path:**
  `source → (per play: filter, pan) → layer gain → bus (beds, birds,
  thunder) → master → output`.
  The 10 Hz update sets layer gains with `setTargetAtTime` (τ 0.15 s). The
  equal-power easing happens in `mix.js`, in power, not in the audio graph.
- Files load from `import.meta.env.BASE_URL + 'audio/'`, so GitHub Pages
  works.
- Flocks stay silent (architecture §2). `chirpFlock()` stays as a no-op
  hook, ready for a flock call later.

## Dev

- `SoundDirector.update` already runs every frame and throttles to 10 Hz,
  as the old one did.
- **Starting a bed:** `source.start(0, offset)` with `loop = true`, where
  `offset` is random for a bed coming back after it stopped. A bed stops
  3 s after its weight reaches zero.
- **Phrases are scheduled ahead** on the audio clock (`start(when)`), so
  they stay in time even if a frame stutters.
- The old synth chirps go: they were placeholders, and the architecture
  says flocks are silent for now.

## QA

- **Unit tests** (`tests/audio.test.mjs`, node only):
  - every scene has a mix, and every id in the mix, event pools and thunder
    exists in `manifest.json`;
  - every manifest file exists on disk;
  - the manifest licences are on the D-6.6 allowlist;
  - equal-power easing keeps total power within ±1 dB through a crossfade,
    and reaches its targets;
  - the bird scheduler: no events in wind, rain or night; a seeded mean
    interval within 20% of config; never the same file twice in a row;
    koel phrases have 2–4 calls;
  - thunder: none outside rain; the first roll inside the window; gaps
    ≥ 25 s; gain and cut-off fall as distance grows; the close file only
    under 5 km, once per visit.
- **In the browser:** sound on → the context runs and the current scene's
  files load first; force rain, then "Thunder now" → a roll plays; no
  console errors; the production build passes.
- **By ear:** Tony's listening review (feedback after this pass).
